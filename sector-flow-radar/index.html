How to read this — three numbers, that's it▾

1 · Vs week and vs month

Today's volume divided by a normal day — measured two ways. Vs week uses the last 7 days, vs month uses the last 30. 1.0 is normal, 2.0 is double. Both high = a real move with fuel behind it. Month high but week flat = it has been hot all week already, you are late. Week high but month flat = something just started today.

2 · Share change

The slice of all altcoin volume this sector is taking today, minus its average slice over the last 7 days. Shown in percentage points. Money has to come from somewhere, so a sector gaining share means another one is losing it. This is the rotation signal.

3 · Participation

How many coins in the sector are individually busy (their own volume above 1.5× normal). High volume with low participation means one coin is pumping and the sector is not really moving. This column kills most false alarms.

Reading the verdict

Volume alone never says who is buying. The verdict pairs volume with the sector's volume-weighted price move: heavy volume with price up is money coming in, heavy volume with price down is people getting out. Open any row to see the coins driving it.
